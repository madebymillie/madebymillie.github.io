About the artist

Millie is a 17-year-old artist who was born in Australia. She loves art, and she’ll make it at any size, from small sketches to massive paintings.

She’s also a hard-working student with a scholarship, and she puts plenty of time and effort into every piece she makes. As a young artist, Millie’s big goal is to get her art out into the world and up on people’s walls. If you have an idea, she’d love to bring it to life.
